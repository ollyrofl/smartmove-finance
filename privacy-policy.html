Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Smartmove Finance collects, uses, stores, discloses, and protects personal and credit information. It reflects our commitment to handling your information carefully, transparently, and in line with the Australian Privacy Principles and other applicable laws.

Our commitment to protecting your privacy

We understand how important it is to protect your personal information. This policy sets out how Smartmove Finance handles personal and credit information, why we collect it, how we use it, and how we work to protect it.

We are committed to handling personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 and any other relevant laws that apply to the services we provide.

Personal information

When we refer to personal information, we mean information or an opinion about an identified individual, or an individual who is reasonably identifiable. Depending on your circumstances, this may include your name, date of birth, residential address, email address, phone number, occupation, identification information, account details, and supporting documents.

If you apply for finance through us, we may also collect information about your residential history, employment details, earnings, liabilities, dependants, and other information needed to assess and support an application.

We may also collect credit information used to assess your eligibility for finance, including outstanding credit arrangements, repayment history information, and default information where relevant.

Why we collect your personal information

We collect, hold, use, and disclose personal information for purposes connected with our business and the services we provide. This may include:

  • assessing finance applications and helping manage finance arrangements
  • maintaining and managing our relationship with you
  • processing payments and administration related to our services
  • evaluating broker, contractor, or employment enquiries where applicable
  • direct marketing where permitted by law

We may also disclose information to organisations that provide products or services connected with the work we do, including lenders, insurers, valuers, accountants, brokers, credit reporting bodies, and other professional or service partners.

How we collect your personal information

Where reasonable and practical, we collect personal information directly from you. This may happen when you contact us, complete forms, provide supporting documents, or engage with us about finance options.

In some situations, we may also collect information from third parties where it is reasonably necessary or you have authorised us to do so. This can include lenders, mortgage brokers, accountants, lawyers, employers, referees, identity verification services, and credit reporting bodies.

When we may disclose personal information

We may disclose your personal information where reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Depending on your circumstances, this may include disclosure to:

  • prospective funders, lenders, aggregators, and intermediaries
  • credit reporting bodies and other third-party suppliers
  • printing, mailing, cloud, call centre, data, and information infrastructure providers
  • brokers, accountants, lawyers, valuers, insurers, and other professional representatives
  • people or entities where you have given consent
  • regulators, government bodies, or other parties where disclosure is required or authorised by law

Before disclosing information, we take reasonable steps to satisfy ourselves that the receiving organisation is committed to protecting personal information appropriately, or that you have consented to the disclosure. Some information may be stored using cloud-based services or handled by support providers located outside Australia.

Credit information

We may exchange credit information for the purpose of assessing an application for finance and managing that finance. Credit information may include credit liability information, repayment history information, information about the type of finance you have applied for, defaults, payment information, and court proceedings information where relevant.

We may exchange this information with credit reporting bodies and use it to assess your creditworthiness, assess your application, and help manage credit arrangements. If you fail to meet payment obligations or commit a serious credit infringement, this may be disclosed to a credit reporting body where permitted by law.

You may request access to, and correction of, your credit information. Credit reporting bodies may also use information for pre-screening credit offers, which you may opt out of directly with them. If you believe you are a victim of fraud, you may ask a credit reporting body not to use or disclose your information for a temporary period as allowed by law.

Direct marketing

We may use your personal information to provide you with information about products, services, updates, or opportunities that may be relevant to you. This may include service-related communications and marketing materials where permitted by law.

You may opt out of receiving marketing communications at any time, free of charge. If a message is sent by email, you may also be able to use the unsubscribe function included in that message.

Access and correction

You may request access to the personal or credit information we hold about you, and you may ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, incomplete, out of date, irrelevant, or misleading. We may need to verify your identity before responding to your request.

We aim to respond within a reasonable timeframe, and in many cases within 30 days. In some situations, the law may allow us to refuse access or decline a correction request in whole or in part. If that happens, we will explain the basis of the decision.

Government identifiers and sensitive information

Where we collect government-related identifiers, such as tax file numbers, we only use or disclose them as required or authorised by law. We do not adopt government identifiers as our own identifiers.

We will only collect sensitive information, such as health information or information about racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, union membership, sexual orientation, or criminal records, with your consent and where it is reasonably necessary or otherwise permitted by law.

How safe and secure is your personal information?

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification, or disclosure. Information may be stored in paper and electronic form, including in secure cloud-based systems used in the delivery of our services.

Doing business without identifying you

Where lawful and practicable, we may allow you to deal with us without identifying yourself, such as when making a general enquiry. In many cases, however, we will need identifying information to assess finance options, comply with legal obligations, or provide our services properly.

Complaints and further information

If you are dissatisfied with how we have handled your personal information or believe we have not complied with the Privacy Act, please contact us first so we can investigate. We aim to acknowledge privacy complaints promptly and provide a substantive response within a reasonable timeframe.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may be able to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes to this policy

We may review and update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, business practices, services, and legal requirements. The latest version published on this page will apply from the date it is posted.
