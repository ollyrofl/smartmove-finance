Stamp Duty Calculator

Estimate stamp duty for every Australian state and territory. Includes first home buyer concessions and all upfront purchase costs.

What you need to know

  • ✓Stamp duty varies significantly by state
  • ✓First home buyers get concessions in most states
  • ✓Investors pay the full rate in all states
  • ✓Paid at settlement — must be budgeted upfront

Stamp duty (transfer duty) is one of the biggest upfront costs in a property purchase. Budget for it alongside your deposit, legal fees, and building inspection.
