Being self-employed is a strength. We help lenders see it that way.

Full-Doc & Low-Doc Options

If you have two years of tax returns, we go full-doc with mainstream lenders. If your financials are limited, we access low-doc products through specialist lenders — with competitive rates.

Income Add-Back Strategy

Depreciation, motor vehicle expenses, and other legitimate deductions can be added back to your assessable income. We know exactly what lenders allow and how to document it correctly.

Specialist Lender Access

Some lenders actively compete for self-employed borrowers. We know who they are and how their assessment criteria work — giving you more options than a standard bank application would.

Application Presentation

How you present a self-employed application matters enormously. We structure your financials, write the supporting narrative, and choose the right lender for your specific income profile.

How it works

We simplify self-employed lending into a clear, managed process.

1

Free consultation & income assessment

We review your income structure, tax returns, and business financials to assess your borrowing capacity and identify the best path forward.

2

Lender matching

We identify lenders whose self-employed assessment criteria best suit your specific income type, structure, and documentation availability.

3

Application preparation

We prepare your full application with a clear income narrative, add-back calculations, and supporting documentation — all presented to maximise approval chances.

4

Approval & settlement

We manage follow-up with the lender, respond to any additional queries, and coordinate through to approval and settlement.
