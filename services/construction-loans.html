What We Do

Construction finance is complex — we simplify every stage of it.

A construction loan works differently to a standard home loan. Funds are released in progress draws aligned to your builder's schedule — slab, frame, lock-up, fit-out, and completion. We manage the lender relationship throughout your entire build so you can focus on the exciting decisions.

Progressive draw-down structure — only pay interest on what's been drawn at each stage
We review builder contracts and progress payment schedules before lodgement

How We Help

Built to handle every
stage of your build

Progressive Draw-Down

Your loan funds are released at each build stage — slab, frame, lock-up, fit-out, and completion. You only pay interest on drawn funds, keeping your costs manageable during the build.

Land & Build Bundle

We can bundle your land purchase and construction into a single facility, simplifying your finance and reducing the number of transactions you need to manage.

Builder & Contract Review

We review your fixed-price building contract and progress payment schedule before we lodge the loan — lenders have specific requirements and we make sure everything aligns.

Completion Rollover

At the end of your build, we convert your construction facility into a standard home loan with the rate, features, and term that best suit your situation at that point.

How construction finance works

From land purchase to keys-in-hand, we manage the finance at every stage.

1

Initial consultation & pre-approval

We assess your situation, confirm your budget, and arrange pre-approval so you can sign a building contract with confidence.

2

Contract review & loan lodgement

We review your fixed-price building contract, prepare the full application, and lodge with the chosen lender.

3

Land settlement

If you're purchasing land and building, we coordinate land settlement first, then activate the construction component of your facility.

4

Progress draws during build

We submit each progress draw request to the lender on your behalf at each stage of the build — slab, frame, lock-up, fit-out, and completion.

5

Final inspection & rollover

Once the build is complete and the lender's final inspection is done, we convert your facility to your ongoing home loan. Move in and enjoy.
