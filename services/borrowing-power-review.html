What's Included

A genuine assessment —
not just a calculator

No Credit Impact

Our initial review uses a soft assessment only — no credit enquiry is placed on your file until you decide to proceed with a formal application.

Detailed Breakdown

We break down exactly how lenders assess your income, expenses, and existing debts — so you understand your borrowing capacity and why it is what it is.

Lender Comparison

Different lenders calculate borrowing power differently. We show you how multiple lenders from our panel would each assess your situation — giving you the full picture.

Action Plan

If your borrowing power isn't where you'd like it, we identify specific steps — paying down a credit card, restructuring debts, or timing your application — that can improve it.

How it works

A clear picture of your borrowing power in just a few days — no stress, no commitment.

1

Tell us about your situation

Share your income, expenses, existing debts, deposit amount, and property goals. Takes about 15 minutes — via phone, video, or our contact form.

2

We assess across lenders

We run your figures through our lender panel to identify your borrowing range across multiple lenders — not just one bank's formula.

3

We present your results

We walk you through your borrowing range, explain what drives it, and identify any steps that could improve your position before you apply.

4

Search with confidence

Armed with a clear budget, you can start your property search knowing exactly what's realistic — and be ready to move quickly when the right property comes up.
