How We Help

Everything you need
in your corner

Grants & Schemes

We identify every government grant, concession, and scheme you're eligible for — First Home Owner Grant, stamp duty exemptions, and the Home Guarantee Scheme.

Low Deposit Options

Purchase with as little as 5% deposit through government-backed schemes. We structure your application to maximise your chances of approval without paying unnecessary LMI.

Lender Comparison

We search 40+ lenders — not just the big four — to find a rate and loan structure that suits your specific deposit, income type, and property goals.

Paperwork & Lodgement

We prepare your complete application, gather supporting documents, and liaise with the lender on your behalf so you can focus on finding the right property.

How it works

Five steps from where you are now to the front door of your first home.

1

Book a free consultation

Tell us about your goals, current savings, and situation. We'll assess your borrowing capacity with no obligation and no credit impact.

2

We compare the market

We search across 40+ lenders and identify all grants and schemes you qualify for, then present your options in plain English with clear tradeoffs.

3

We handle the paperwork

Once you choose a lender, we prepare your full application, gather supporting documents, and lodge everything on your behalf.

4

Pre-approval & property search

With pre-approval in hand, you search with confidence knowing exactly what you can spend. We stay on call as you find the right home.

5

Formal approval & settlement

We coordinate with your solicitor and lender through to formal approval and a smooth, on-time settlement. Then we celebrate with you.
