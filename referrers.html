How It Works

A simple referral flow that keeps everyone informed.

You can introduce clients in whatever way feels most natural, whether that is a direct email, a warm handover call, or a message asking us to reach out. From there, we take care of the lending side while keeping the process calm, transparent, and respectful of your relationship.

01

Make the introduction

Send through the client details, or connect us directly once you know they are ready for a lending conversation.

02

We assess the scenario

We learn what the client is trying to achieve, review the lending position, and compare lender options that fit the brief.

03

We keep the process moving

From documentation to lodgement, follow-up, approval, and settlement, we manage the detail and keep communication clear.
